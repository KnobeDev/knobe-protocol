# A KNOBE you can test

This file is a KNOBE. The text you are reading now is the part made for people. The block at the bottom is a sealed record made for machines: it says who made the file, under what license, and what not to read into it. The seal is a hash of that record, and the readable text has a hash of its own inside the record.

**Verify.** A verified check shows that the record matches the seal it carries.

**Tamper.** The Tamper button on knobe.org changes one letter in the sealed record and checks again. The check fails, because the record no longer matches its seal.

**Edit the readable text.** If you change these words and leave the record alone, the check says so separately: the record still matches its seal, but the readable text no longer matches the hash sealed with it.

**What the check cannot tell you.** Anyone who edits a file can seal it again, and the new seal will check out. So the check means most when you already hold the hash, from a source you trust, and compare it with this file's hash. A verified seal does not show that these words are correct, or that the person named in the record wrote them. Integrity, not truth.
