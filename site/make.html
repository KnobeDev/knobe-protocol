I make or teach with knowledge

Keep the terms with the work.

You already carry the knowing in your head: who gave consent, what a transcript may and may not be used for, which parts of a summary were yours and which an assistant drafted, where a translation lost something. The trouble is that none of it travels with the file. A KNOBE lets you write those terms into the file itself, in plain words, with a seal that shows whether they have been altered since. A student, a colleague, or an AI assistant that receives the file receives the terms too. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for, and KNOBE fits the workflow you already have, because a sealed file is still plain-text Markdown. The materials below are what exists today; simpler integrations are in development, built to keep every step transparent and under your control.
