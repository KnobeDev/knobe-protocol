I decide

Integrity, not truth. Advisory, not enforced. Independent, not institutional.

KNOBE makes three narrow promises and refuses several larger ones. It promises that a file can carry its own declared origin, history, limits and terms in plain text with no platform required; that a local check can show whether that declaration has been altered since it was sealed; and that the file stays readable in any editor whether or not anyone ever checks it. It does not claim the content is true, that the declaration is honest, that it can detect undeclared AI text, or that a failed check stops anyone from reading. It is an open protocol under an independent steward, not a product and not a UC Davis standard. The obligation map on the policy page says exactly what a record supplies toward each emerging requirement, and what it does not.
