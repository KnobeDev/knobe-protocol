What KNOBE claims, and what it does not.

A policy reader does not want the spec. They want to know what the thing claims, what it refuses to claim, and which obligations it bears on. This page is those three things.

Claims

  • A knowledge file can carry its own declared origin, history, limits and terms of use in plain text, with no platform required.
  • A verifier, running locally, can show whether that declared record has changed since it was sealed.
  • The same file stays readable in any editor or screen reader whether or not it is ever verified.

Does not claim

  • That the content is true. A seal proves the record is intact, not accurate.
  • That declarations are honest. Attribution is a good-faith declaration, and the seal cannot detect a lie told at sealing time.
  • That it identifies AI-generated text nobody declared. KNOBE is a declaration format, not a detector. It is complementary to watermarking, not a substitute for it.
  • That the readable body is always sealed. The body's integrity is checked only when the optional body hash is present. A copied excerpt does not inherit the original's seal, and a file whose payload has been stripped does not announce the fact to a recipient who never saw the original.
  • That verification is enforcement. A failed check is a reason to inspect, not a barrier to reading.
  • That it carries any institutional endorsement. It is an independent open protocol, not a UC Davis standard or product.

Obligation map

Checked against the decree text (Gazzetta Ufficiale n. 234, 8 October 2026): the human-decision rule and the explanation right are confirmed at article 41. One element reported in secondary coverage, the contribution of human intervention, does not appear in article 41(3) and is marked in its row. This note comes off after David's final read.

The case to hold in mind: a manager writes notes on one quarter of someone’s work, marked draft, with one line the employee disputes. HR summarizes the notes. An AI drafts a reference from the summary. A hiring decision rests on the reference. By the fourth step a disputed draft about one quarter reads as a settled judgment about a person, and nothing in the final document says so. That is the situation the first three rows below now give a worker in Italy the right to have explained.

Each row names an obligation now in force or in policy, and says precisely what a KNOBE record supplies toward it. The honest verb is supplies, not satisfies: the obligations bind people and organizations, and KNOBE records artifacts. A compliant explanation draws on the record; the record does not make the explanation.
