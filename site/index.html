Information is portable. The knowing that makes it knowledge is not.

A .knobe.md is a plain-text file: portable, interoperable, readable by people, machines and screen readers. But a portable file is not yet portable knowledge. A KNOBE also carries the knowing, who made it, under what terms, with what limits, so what arrives downstream is knowledge rather than loose content.

known+knowing=knowledge

The known: the information in front of you. The knowing: who made it, under what terms, with what limits.

Severed knowledge.

In Severance, a person's working self has no memory of the life that produced them. Knowledge now moves the same way: the facts clock in, nearly everything that would let you judge them stays outside the door, and no accounting is kept of what was left behind. A KNOBE refuses that severance: the conditions a piece of knowledge was made under travel with it, system to system.

The longer argument, in one essay: Knowledge should outlive its platform.

One file, three layers.

A single plain-text file. The top is for people, the bottom for machines, and none of it needs an account or a network to read or to check.

LAYER 1

Words anyone can read.

The document itself, in ordinary Markdown. Opens in any editor or screen reader.

LAYER 2

A record a machine can read.

Who made it, from what, under what terms, with what limits, and whether AI was involved.

LAYER 3

A seal.

A check that shows whether that record has changed since it was made. It runs in your browser and uploads nothing.

A verified seal proves the record is intact. It does not prove the record is true. That distinction is the whole design.

Where the project stands

    
Year two of development. The protocol is small on purpose, it works today, and the parts that do not exist yet are listed as not existing. As of November 2026.

  

  

    

      
ComponentState

      
        
Specificationv1.0, frozen, published 2026. Changes are recorded in the open in the errata.

        
ConformanceNine test vectors covering the full conformance surface.

        
ImplementationsJavaScript, Python and Go. The JavaScript and Python verifiers are checked against each other across the published corpus.

        
Agent accessknobe-mcp v0.1.1 on npm: five tools, runs locally, no telemetry.

        
Browser toolsStudio, Grove and Lens. No account; nothing is uploaded.

        
Institutional pilotsUC Davis pilots are planned. None are active yet.

        
Known limitsDocumented in the threat model and restated as the six refusals on the policy page.

        
StewardshipIndependent. Protocol CC BY 4.0, code Apache-2.0.
