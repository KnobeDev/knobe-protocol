Knowledge travels. The knowing that produced it often doesn't.

When a transcript, a lesson, a research note or an AI summary moves from one place to another, the facts tend to arrive. Who made it, under what terms, with what limits, and where judgment entered tend not to. KNOBE is a plain-text file format that lets a piece of knowledge carry its own knowing wherever it goes, including through AI.

Without KNOBE: at the transcript, four of four conditions are present. At the AI abstract, two of four. At the newsletter and the grant report, none. With KNOBE: four of four at every station, and the file carries a seal.

A graduate student records an oral history. The transcript carries consent terms, a do-not-quote request, and the notes that explain them. A semester later an excerpt goes into an AI assistant for an abstract. The abstract feeds a newsletter. A line from the newsletter lands in a grant report. Every step was reasonable. By the fourth, nobody downstream knows the terms ever existed.

The white paper calls this compression without portable interpretive obligation. Most people call it losing the thread.

Severed knowledge.

In Severance, a person's working self has no memory of the life that produced them. Most knowledge now moves the same way: the known arrives with no memory of the knowing. The facts clock in. Everything that would let you judge them stays outside the door. A KNOBE is a file that refuses the severance.

The longer argument, in one essay: Knowledge should outlive its platform.

One file, three layers.

A KNOBE is a single plain-text file. The top is for people. The bottom is for machines. Nothing in it needs an account, a platform, or a network connection to read or to check.

LAYER 1

Words anyone can read.

The document itself, in ordinary Markdown. Opens in any editor or screen reader.

LAYER 2

A record a machine can read.

Who made it, from what, under what terms, with what limits, and whether AI was involved.

LAYER 3

A seal.

A check that shows whether that record has changed since it was made. It runs in your browser and uploads nothing.

A verified seal proves the record is intact. It does not prove the record is true. That distinction is the whole design.
